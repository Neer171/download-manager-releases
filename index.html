What it does

Faster downloads

Each file is fetched over up to 32 connections at once, so big downloads finish sooner.

Pause and resume

Stop any time, even restart your computer. Lost connection? It waits and continues by itself.

Videos up to 8K

A "Download video" button on players, for YouTube and 1,000+ sites. Pick the quality, or just the audio.

Works with your browser

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc and Firefox. Downloads go to the app automatically.

Copy a link, done

Copy a download link anywhere and the app offers to download it.

Playlists and clips

Choose videos from a playlist, or save just part of a video.

Schedule and organise

Download overnight, sort files into folders by type, unzip archives, set a speed limit.

Make videos smaller

Right-click a video to shrink it for WhatsApp, email or Discord. The original stays.

Instagram posts

Save every photo and video of a post, using your Instagram login in your browser.

From and to your phone

Scan a code to send a link from your phone, or to get a downloaded file onto it.

Convert and organise

Turn a video into MP3, save each website's downloads to its own folder, and see previews.

Watch your data

See how much you downloaded this month and set a limit for mobile data plans.

Looks after your laptop

Waits for disk space instead of filling the disk, and can pause on battery or a phone hotspot.

In your language

English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Indonesian.

Updates itself

New versions install automatically. Every update is digitally signed and checked.

Install

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Download Manager into Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS says it can't check the app: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
  3. Click Extension in the app to add it to your browser.

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu › About This Mac: "Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.

Windows 10 / 11

  1. Run the Setup .exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": More info › Run anyway.
  2. Click through the installer; no administrator rights needed.
  3. Click Extension in the app to add it to your browser.

Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Mint…)

  1. Download the .deb for your computer (amd64 for most PCs).
  2. Run sudo apt install ./download-manager_*.deb
  3. Open Download Manager from your app menu.

Private and safe

Stays on your computer

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The app only talks to the sites you download from and checks for its own updates.

Checked downloads

Downloaded files are marked as coming from the internet, so your system still checks them before they run. On Windows, Microsoft Defender scans every finished download.

Copy protection respected

DRM-protected video (Netflix and similar) isn't downloaded. Only save content you have the right to.

Found a security problem? Report it privately.
